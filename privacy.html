Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Exotix creators LLC, trading as Vibrae ("Vibrae", "we", "us"), handles personal information collected through the website vibrae.vip (the "Site"). Exotix creators LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company with its registered office at 30 N Gould St Ste N, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States.

1. Information we collect

The Site is an informational website. It has no user accounts, no forms and no comment features.

  • Information you send us. If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the information you choose to provide (for example your name, email address and the content of your message).
  • Technical information. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type to operate and secure the hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which may log your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on the Site.

2. How we use information

We use the information you send us to respond to your enquiry, to evaluate a potential working relationship, and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

3. Creators we work with

If you become a creator represented by Vibrae, we collect additional information under a separate written agreement, including identity documents required for age verification and platform compliance. That information is handled under the terms of that agreement and is not covered by this Site policy.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers that help us operate (for example email hosting), when required by law, or with your consent. We do not share it with advertisers.

5. Retention

Email correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry and to meet legal and accounting requirements, after which it is deleted.

6. International transfers

We are a United States company operated by a remote team. Information you send us may be processed in the United States and in the countries where our team members are located.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent to its processing. To exercise these rights, email hello@vibrae.vip. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

The Site and our services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current version.
