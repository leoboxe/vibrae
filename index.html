Our standards

The subscription-content industry is regulated by platform rules, payment-network rules and the law. We take all three seriously.

  • Adults only. We work exclusively with verified adult creators (18+). Every creator completes government-ID and age verification before onboarding.
  • Consent and ownership. Creators own their content and their accounts. Nothing is published, edited or promoted without the creator's approval.
  • Platform compliance. We operate within the terms of service of every platform we work on and the acceptable-use policies of their payment partners.
  • No platform, no payments from fans. Vibrae does not operate a content platform, does not sell content and does not process payments from fans. All fan transactions take place on the third-party platforms; Vibrae is paid a management fee by the creator.
  • Content protection. We monitor for unauthorised redistribution and file DMCA takedowns on the creator's behalf.
  • No adult content on this website. This site describes our services only.
